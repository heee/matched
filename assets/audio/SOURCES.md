# Audio sources

`wooden-piece-click-a.wav` and `wooden-piece-click-b.wav` are excerpts from
“Board Game Pieces.WAV” by Freesound user **taure**. The source is a field
recording of wooden board-game pieces being set down and moved on a board.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/taure/sounds/555190/
- License: [Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Changes: two isolated impacts were excerpted from the low-quality preview;
  the game cue crops, fades, level-matches, and sequences those recordings.

The other material cues use excerpts from these CC0 Freesound recordings:

- Stone — [“Slate hit” by Sadiquecat](https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/801971/), two slate rocks striking.
- Resin — [“Lego Click (short)” by ImmergoMedia](https://freesound.org/people/ImmergoMedia/sounds/670000/), two hard plastic pieces tapped together.
- Bamboo — [“Bambú.wav” by JDax88](https://freesound.org/people/JDax88/sounds/646893/), consecutive bamboo impacts.
- Bone — [“bone shell” by blukotek](https://freesound.org/people/blukotek/sounds/249319/), bone/shell pieces mixed like dice.
- Porcelain — [“Cup and Saucer” by bowlingballout](https://freesound.org/people/bowlingballout/sounds/568757/), porcelain set onto porcelain.

Each is licensed [Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changes are limited to isolating impacts, stereo placement, fades, level matching, and sequencing two hits into a match cue.
